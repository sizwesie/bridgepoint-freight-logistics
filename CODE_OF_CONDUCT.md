# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and users.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassing language or personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Conduct unbecoming of a professional

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove comments, commits, code, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
